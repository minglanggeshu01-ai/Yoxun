US Last-Mile Delivery Tricycle Battery Program

An American last-mile logistics company selected our IP67 waterproof 60V 166Ah batteries to power their fleet of electric delivery tricycles across multiple US cities. The key requirement was all-weather reliability — these vehicles operate year-round in rain, snow, and extreme temperature swings from Chicago winters to Phoenix summers.

Our fully sealed IP67 enclosure design exceeded expectations, with 1,500 units deployed and zero field failures reported in 18+ months of continuous daily operation. Energy Storage Hong Kong 500 Units

Hong Kong Commercial Building ESS Retrofit

A major Hong Kong property developer undertook a portfolio-wide energy efficiency initiative, selecting our 20kWh all-in-one energy storage systems for 500 commercial units across their Kowloon and Hong Kong Island properties. Power Battery Ghana 100 Units

Ghana Industrial Forklift Battery Replacement

A Ghanaian logistics company serving West Africa's largest port operations made the strategic decision to replace their entire forklift fleet's lead-acid batteries with our 24V 200Ah LiFePO4 deep cycle units. The primary drivers were eliminating daily watering maintenance, reducing lengthy charging downtime, and extending operational lifespan in Accra's hot, humid warehouse environment.

100 units were deployed across their three warehouse facilities. The transition cut charging time by 70% — from 8+ hours to under 2.5 hours — and eliminated all watering and acid-related maintenance. With a projected 5+ year service life versus just 2 years with lead-acid, the client expects full ROI within 14 months. They have already recommended our solution to two partner logistics firms in the region.
