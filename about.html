Who We Are

Your Trusted Battery Manufacturing Partner

Founded in 2001, Huizhou Yixun Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is an integrated industrial and trade lithium battery enterprise. Headquartered in Huizhou, Guangdong — the heart of the Greater Bay Area — we operate a 5,000sqm modern manufacturing facility with fully automated production lines for cell sorting, laser welding, BMS testing, and pack assembly.

Our core product lines serve industries ranging from power tools and smart homes to e-mobility, telecom, and renewable energy. With ISO9001 certification and CE/RoHS/UN38.3/IEC62619 compliance, our products are trusted in 30+ countries across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Australia.
