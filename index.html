About Yixun

Your Trusted Battery Manufacturing Partner

Huizhou Yixun Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. is an integrated industrial and trade lithium battery enterprise, headquartered in Huizhou, Guangdong — the heart of the Greater Bay Area. We specialize in lithium batteries, energy storage, power batteries, and outdoor power solutions, delivering safe and reliable battery solutions to B2B clients worldwide.

With a 5,000㎡ modern manufacturing facility and ISO9001 certification, our products are CE, RoHS, and UN38.3 certified, exported to 60+ countries across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
